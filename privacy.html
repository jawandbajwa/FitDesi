Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

FitDesi is a personal fitness app made by one person for family use. This policy explains exactly what data is stored, where, and how to delete it.

1. What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive (from Google):

  • Your Google display name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo URL

When you use the app, we store (your inputs):

  • Your fitness profile: age, gender, weight, height, body-fat measurements, fitness goal, activity level, weight unit preference
  • Your meal logs: dates, meals, and macro totals
  • Your workout cycle: start date, split selection, completed sets and reps
  • Your weight + body-fat history (if you log progress)
  • Your personal recipes (if you submit any)
  • Your AI coach conversation history (last 16 messages, for context)
  • Your theme preference (stored locally on your device only)

We never collect: location, contacts, photos, payment information, browsing history, advertising IDs.

2. Where it's stored

All your data is stored in Google Firestore under your Google account UID. The database is owned by the developer (jawandbajwa@gmail.com) and hosted on Google Cloud (Firebase). Firestore security rules ensure only you and the admin can read your data.

Your theme preference and small caches are stored in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB — they never leave your device.

3. Who can see your data

  • You — when signed in
  • The admin (jawandbajwa@gmail.com) — has read access to all user profiles to manage the app. Admin actions are logged.
  • Google — as the cloud host, subject to Google Cloud's security

We never sell, share, or rent your data. We never run ads.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication only. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Worker proxy (fitdesi-gemini.jawandbajwa.workers.dev) — relays AI-coach prompts to Google Gemini. Cloudflare does not retain message content.
  • Google Gemini API — processes your coach chat prompts (only when you use the coach). Subject to Google's API terms. We do not send your name or email to Gemini — only your profile macros, recent meals, and the conversation.
  • jsDelivr CDN — serves Chart.js library for progress charts.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter font.

5. Your rights

You can:

  • View your data — it's all visible in the app while signed in
  • Edit any data — Profile, Tracker, Exercise pages
  • Delete your account — Profile → Account → Delete Account. This purges all your Firestore data immediately and irreversibly.
  • Export your data — contact jawandbajwa@gmail.com (manual export, not yet automated)
  • Revoke Google access — at myaccount.google.com/permissions

6. Cookies

FitDesi does not use tracking cookies. The only browser storage we use is for authentication tokens (managed by Firebase) and your theme/cache (in localStorage and IndexedDB). No cookies are sent to advertisers.

7. Children

FitDesi is not directed at children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided data, contact us and it will be deleted.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes too. Significant changes will be announced in the in-app changelog.

9. Contact

Questions about your data? Email jawandbajwa@gmail.com or open a GitHub issue.
